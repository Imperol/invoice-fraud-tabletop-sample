One useful reflection

Matching invoice details do not establish that the new bank instructions are genuine.

  • Does your verification route depend on the suspicious message?
  • Could everyone assume someone else has stopped the payment?
  • What evidence would change your decision?

This is a discussion prompt, not a pass/fail assessment or proof that a payment is safe.
